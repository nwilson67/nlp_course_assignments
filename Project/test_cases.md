### Try these five test cases!

#### Search the web and tell me where to catch mewtwo (Web Search)
Answer: You can catch Mewtwo in the original 151 Pokémon games in Cerulean Cave (also known as the Unknown Dungeon), which is located in Cerulean City. You'll find the cave in the top left corner of the town after traveling north to Route 24 and surfing.

#### Which moves does mewtwo have? (Database Query)
Answer: Mewtwo has the following moves: Fire Blast, Solar Beam, Rock Slide, and Thunder.
